How the club works

From campus curiosity to credible work.

Members get a place to find problems, build with others, get feedback, and create evidence that an idea deserves another step.

01

Find the problem

Turn observations from campus, industry, and everyday life into clearly framed problems worth investigating.

02

Build a test

Run small experiments, prototype quickly, and learn before wasting weeks polishing an unproven idea.

03

Share the evidence

Pitch to peers, mentors, and practitioners with what you learned—not just what you hope is true.
