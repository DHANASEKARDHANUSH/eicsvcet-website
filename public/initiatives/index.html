What we run

Formats that make learning unavoidable.

Every initiative is designed around a concrete output: a prototype, a pitch, a set of interviews, a research note, or a decision backed by evidence.

01

Build sprints

Short, focused cycles where mixed teams take an idea from problem statement to a demo they can show.

02

Founder rooms

Small conversations with founders, operators, investors, and alumni.

03

Research jams

Peer sessions for validating assumptions, reading papers, analyzing markets, and turning observations into experiments.

04

Pitch clinics

Practice a pitch in front of people who will challenge the logic, not just clap for the confidence.

05

Campus problems

Identify real friction in student life and prototype practical solutions that can be piloted locally.

06

Skill studios

Hands-on sessions on product discovery, no-code tools, coding, design, finance, storytelling, and go-to-market.

Your first step

Bring a problem, not a polished pitch.

New members do not need a startup idea. Start with a problem you keep noticing. The club can help you investigate it and find collaborators.
