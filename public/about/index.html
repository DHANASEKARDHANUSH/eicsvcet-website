About the club

Curiosity is easy. Building is the discipline.

EIC brings together students who want to explore entrepreneurship and innovation through action: research, prototyping, events, mentorship, and honest feedback.

Our mission

Make experimentation normal on campus.

We want students to leave university knowing how to move from vague idea → clear problem → testable assumption → working prototype → informed decision.

That means welcoming beginners, giving experienced members harder problems, and treating failed experiments as useful evidence rather than embarrassment.

Our values

Four things we refuse to compromise on.

01

Do the work

Ideas are cheap. We care about experiments, prototypes, interviews, demos, and results.

02

Stay intellectually honest

Strong members are willing to change their mind when evidence says the original idea was wrong.

03

Build with people

Founders need designers, engineers, operators, researchers, storytellers, and domain experts.

04

Leave the room better

Mentorship, documentation, and feedback should compound so the next member starts from a stronger base.
