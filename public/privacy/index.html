Privacy

Your application is not a marketing list.

This sample notice explains how the club website handles information submitted through the membership form. The club should have the institution approve the final version before launch.

What we collect

Name, college email, phone number, register number, department, year, selected interest areas, and the short motivation response you submit.

Why we collect it

The club uses the information to process membership applications, contact applicants about the application, and organize club participation.

Where it goes

The sample implementation stores submissions in a PostgreSQL database on the club server. It does not load third-party analytics, advertising scripts, or external web fonts.

What to finalize

Before launch, the club should define an approved retention period, who can access applications, the official data controller/contact, and the institution’s required student-data handling rules.
